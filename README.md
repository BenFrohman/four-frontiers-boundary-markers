# four-frontiers-boundary-markers

Lean 4 formalization of machine-checkable boundary markers for four research frontiers: sextic Jacobian Hodge numbers, quintic mirror instantons, incidence dimension for Clemens, and height bounds for curve 3936b1.

## Paper
A Machine-Checked Frontier Reduction and Cohomological Rigidity Equivalence for Clemens' Conjecture.  
The formalization certifies the Frontier Reduction Theorem and the 5-Way Internal Rigidity Equivalence. The Birational Tail Condition and the torsion statement for curve 3936b1 remain open pending green CI.
